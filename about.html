About M Tech

A family business, built on spinning.

We've worked with Pakistan's spinning mills since 1988. Today the office is in Manga Mandi, Lahore, and the machinery godown is in Faisalabad.

Our story

It started with spare parts.

Abdul Rasheed Gill learned the trade on the open-end spinning floors of Karachi. In 1988 he set up M Tech Textile Traders there, selling open-end spinning spare parts to the mills he already knew.

Parts led to machines, machines led to whole mills, and later to importing machinery from abroad. The work itself hasn't changed much: know the machine, value it honestly, and stand behind the deal.

He still runs M Tech, now with his four sons, from the head office in Manga Mandi, Lahore and the godown in Darul Ehsan Town, Faisalabad.

Founder & CEO
