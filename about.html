About M Tech

A family business, built on spinning.

M Tech Textile Traders has served Pakistan's textile industry since 1988, from Manga Mandi in Lahore and a warehouse in Faisalabad.

Our story

Abdul Rasheed Gill learned the trade on the open-end spinning floors of Karachi. In 1988 he founded M Tech Textile Traders in Karachi, starting with open-end spinning spare parts.

The business started with spare parts. It grew into machinery, then complete mills, and then imports from abroad. Through all of it, the work stayed the same: know the machine, value it honestly, and stand behind the deal.

Today M Tech is run by Abdul Rasheed Gill and his four sons, from our head office in Manga Mandi, Lahore, and our warehouse in Darul Ehsan Town, Faisalabad.
